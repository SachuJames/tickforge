# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary)

TickForge is an open research and engineering project. Everyone participating is expected to act professionally and respectfully.

## Our standards

* Be welcoming to new contributors and respectful of differing viewpoints.
* Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
* Focus on the technical merit of ideas, not on the people proposing them.
* Do not harass, insult, or demean other participants.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via a private GitHub message or vulnerability-style private report. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove participants who violate these standards.

This is a deliberately brief adaptation of the Contributor Covenant for a small early-stage project; it will grow with the community.
